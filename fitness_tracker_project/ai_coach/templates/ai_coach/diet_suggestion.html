🥗 Everyday energy

Balanced daily plan

A practical everyday option for general fitness and steady energy.

Breakfast
Oats 50 g + milk 250 ml + banana 1 medium + almonds 10
Mid-morning snack
Apple 1 medium or any seasonal fruit 150 g
Lunch
Rice 1 cup cooked or 2 chapatis + dal 1 cup + paneer 100 g + salad 1 bowl
Evening snack
Curd 150 g or roasted chana 30 g + unsweetened tea
Dinner
2 chapatis + mixed vegetables 1.5 cups + curd 100 g
🔥 Lean & steady

Weight-loss plan

High-fiber, protein-focused meals with controlled portions.

Breakfast
2 eggs or paneer 100 g + 1 slice whole-grain toast + vegetables
Snack
Guava 1 medium or cucumber/carrot sticks 1 bowl
Lunch
2 chapatis + grilled chicken 120 g or tofu 150 g + salad 2 cups
Evening snack
Buttermilk 250 ml or roasted makhana 25 g
Dinner

💪 Training fuel

Muscle-gain plan

Add protein and energy around training while keeping meals balanced.

Breakfast
3 eggs or paneer 150 g + oats 60 g + fruit 1 serving
Post-workout
Milk 300 ml + banana 1 + peanut butter 1 tablespoon
Lunch
Rice 1.5 cups cooked + chicken 150 g or rajma 1 cup + vegetables 1 bowl
Snack
Greek yogurt/curd 200 g + nuts 20 g
Dinner
3 chapatis + fish/chicken 150 g or tofu 180 g + vegetables 1 cup
🌱 Plant-powered

Vegetarian options

Mix and match these vegetarian protein-rich choices.

  • Paneer: 100-150 g per meal
  • Tofu: 150-200 g per meal
  • Dal or rajma: 1-1.5 cups cooked
  • Curd or Greek yogurt: 150-200 g
  • Soy chunks: 40-50 g dry weight
  • Chana or sprouts: 1 cup cooked
🤰 Gentle guidance

Pregnancy-friendly general guidance

Choose gentle, nourishing meals and eat smaller portions more often if needed.

Breakfast
Poha/upma 1 cup + milk 1 glass + fruit 1 serving
Lunch
2 chapatis + dal 1 cup + vegetables 1 cup + curd 1 small bowl
Snack
Fruit 1 serving + nuts 10-15 or roasted chana 25 g
Pregnancy nutrition must be personalized by your doctor or registered dietitian. Avoid unpasteurized foods and any food your clinician has restricted; do not follow calorie-reduction plans during pregnancy without medical advice.
💧 Aim for regular water intake through the day and follow your healthcare professional's advice for your personal needs.
